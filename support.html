What to include

So we can help without a second round of questions, please tell us:

  • The phone number your account is registered to
  • The deal or contract number, if your question is about a specific one
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead
  • A screenshot, if something looked wrong on screen

Common requests

  • A deal is stuck or the other side is not responding. Open the deal in the app and start a dispute. The money stays frozen in escrow until the dispute is resolved.
  • You did not receive the SMS code. Check that the number is entered correctly, wait for the timer to run out, and request the code again.
  • You forgot your PIN. Sign out and sign in again with your phone number — you set a new PIN on every login.
  • You want your data deleted. Write to the address above. See the Privacy Policy for what we keep and for how long.
