Public offer and terms for using the Omon Pay service

Last updated: June 2026

1. General Provisions

1.1. This document is an official public offer governing the relationship between the "Omon Pay" mobile application (hereinafter — the System) and individuals (hereinafter — the User).

1.2. The System provides an Escrow (intermediary/holding) service to securely facilitate transactions between individuals.

1.3. Registering in the System and using it means full and unconditional acceptance of these Terms.

2. Registration and Identity Verification (KYC)

2.1. Only transactions between individuals are permitted in the System. Services for legal entities are not currently supported.

2.2. To conclude a transaction through the System and become a party to it, the User must complete mandatory identity verification (KYC) through the MyID digital identification system. Users who have not verified their identity are prohibited from concluding transactions.

3. Escrow (Holding) System and Payments

3.1. During a transaction, the funds entered by the Buyer are withdrawn through the Click payment system and securely held in the Omon Pay transit account.

3.2. After the Seller fulfills the terms of the transaction and delivers the product or service, the Buyer must confirm the transaction within the application. Once the Buyer confirms, the funds are transferred to the Seller's account.

3.3. Commission terms: Omon Pay charges a commission of a set amount from the transaction sum for the service provided. The commission is paid by the Buyer.

4. Cancellation and Refunds

4.1. If a transaction is canceled by mutual consent of the parties or because the agreed terms were not met, the held funds are returned to the Buyer's account within 1 (one) hour.

4.2. In the event of cancellation, the commission charged to cover the operational costs of the Click payment system and the System is non-refundable to the Buyer.

5. Disputes and Arbitration

5.1. If the Seller claims to have fulfilled their obligations but the Buyer does not confirm this and a dispute arises, the funds for the transaction remain held in between.

5.2. Dispute resolution: The held funds are not paid out to either party (neither the Buyer nor the Seller) and remain blocked in the System until both parties reach a mutual agreement and come to a resolution.

5.3. At the start of each transaction, an electronic contract is automatically generated between the Buyer and the Seller, and this contract serves as the primary legal document in disputes.

6. Prohibited Types of Activity

Concluding transactions for the following products and services through the System is strictly prohibited:

  • Contraband and any items prohibited by law;
  • Alcohol and tobacco products;
  • Gambling and related services;
  • Psychotropic, narcotic, and potent substances;
  • Any other goods and services that contravene the legislation of the Republic of Uzbekistan.
